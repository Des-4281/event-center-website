About Hudson River Bar

Hudson River Bar elevates Manhattan events with a soaring, retractable-glass rooftop wrapped in floor-to-ceiling windows that frame panoramic views of the Hudson River and the USS Intrepid.

Fully climate-controlled, the terrace doubles as a tropical escape—lush greenery, wall art, and island-inspired décor evoke Ibiza, Miami, and Bali year-round.

Two large bars, a chef-driven kitchen, mirrored cocktail tables and stylish lounge furniture, support everything from seated dinners to high-energy receptions.

State-of-the-art S/L/V features—intelligent lighting, a Void® sound system, and a mobile DJ booth outfitted with top Pioneer decks—ensure concert-quality audio and immersive ambiance.

Access to the Rooftop includes two main elevators, several staircases, a dedicated VIP elevator and entryway, and a fully equipped kitchen. The retractable glass rooftop safeguards your event from inclement weather.
