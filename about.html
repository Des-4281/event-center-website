About The Event Studio

Over 40 years of unforgettable performances in the heart of the city.

Our Story

Four Decades of Live Music

Founded in 1984, The Event Studio has hosted over 3,000 performances across every genre imaginable. From intimate classical quartets to sold-out headbanger shows, we've moved every crowd the city has to offer.

What began as a small neighborhood venue has grown into one of the most celebrated performance spaces in the country — without ever losing the energy that made us who we are.

Our Mission

Music That Shapes Lives

To create music experiences that shape people's lives, and create memories for everyone — regardless of age, background, or budget.
